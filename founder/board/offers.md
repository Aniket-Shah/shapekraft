# Offers lens
Score: 5/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **One clear core offer.** "Launch + Automate" has a name, a time limit (3 weeks), a concrete result and a guarantee (90+ mobile Lighthouse, enquiries reach inbox/WhatsApp, or it gets fixed free). That is the right shape.
- **A good wedge in B2B.** Traders, manufacturers and distributors (catalogue/RFQ, auto-quote) have a pain linked to revenue, have money, and can be found on IndiaMART. One live chemical-trading site with RFQ forms is real proof for this segment.
- **Cheap to deliver.** The component library and roughly $65 of direct cost per project (an assumed figure) leave room to add value to the stack without hurting margin.
- **The entry offer is well built.** A free speed scan, then a paid audit credited to the build, lowers the effort for the buyer to say yes.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Competing on price in a commodity market.** The founding US prices ($450–$1,800) sit inside the Fiverr and freelancer range, and the "small business website" market has builders at $10–$39/month. If price is the reason to buy, there is no offer, only a discount. The automation is priced at $700 against $1,500–$12,000 at agencies, so the most valuable part is the most underpriced.
2. **Low likelihood (proof).** There are no published case studies and "Featured Work" is switched off. Likelihood is the weakest part of the value equation right now, and the guarantee promises a technical score, not the result the buyer wants (more enquiries, faster quotes, more deals).
3. **The time promise vs capacity.** A 3-week delivery on 15–20 hours a week, with the real hours per project unknown, puts the guarantee at risk. If one late project breaks the promise, trust breaks too.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Which single buyer has the biggest pain and the most money: Indian B2B traders, or US small businesses? Why serve both at once with 15–20 hours a week?
- What did the chemical-trading client gain in numbers (enquiries, response time, quotes sent) since September? If nobody knows, can it be measured now?
- What is the real number of hours one Launch + Automate project takes?
- Could the B2B + automation package be sold at double the price and still be the obvious choice? What has stopped testing that?
- What does it cost to acquire one client through IndiaMART outreach or partners? (Unknown today.)

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. **Pick one market and one buyer** for the next 90 days (B2B RFQ/catalogue is the strongest candidate on this lens) and stop the generic small-business tiers in the headline.
2. **Sell the outcome, not the pages.** Lead with the automation (e.g. RFQ to quote in minutes); reprice it toward the agency range; add a result-based guarantee next to the Lighthouse one.
3. **Publish the chemical-trading case study** with real before/after numbers before scaling outreach.
4. **Measure real hours on the next 2 projects** and only keep "live in 3 weeks" if the numbers support it.
5. **Make founding scarcity real:** 5 slots, with a public counter and a closing date.
