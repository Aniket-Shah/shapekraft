# Board brief: ShapeKraft as a whole business (12 Nov 2026, round 2)

You are judging **ShapeKraft the studio as a whole business**, not a single offer. Evidence gathered since the first board meeting is included below.

## The business

- **What it is:** a remote web engineering studio (founded 2025) that builds fast, custom websites in Next.js/React/TypeScript (no templates) and adds AI automation, integrations and performance work. It runs under one brand with two domains: shapekraft.in (India, INR) and shapekraft.co (US/global, USD).
- **Who it is for (today):** small and growing businesses that want a custom, fast site; B2B companies (traders, manufacturers, distributors) needing catalogue/RFQ flows and maintenance; teams wanting automation wired into existing tools.
- **What it sells (proposed prices, Nov 2026):**

| Item | India | US/global |
|---|---|---|
| Starter site (5 pages) | ₹15,000 | $750 |
| Growth site (10 pages, CMS, lead capture) | ₹35,000 | $1,500 |
| Studio site (15 pages, own design system) | ₹60,000 | $3,000 |
| RFQ Launch Kit for B2B distributors (catalogue site + "quote desk" that drafts quotes from the client's price list for approval on WhatsApp) | ₹60,000 + ₹5,000/month | priced on a call |
| Quote Desk Pilot (14 days on the client's own price list, credited) | ₹5,000 | — |
| First automation workflow | ₹30,000 | $1,500 |
| Care plans | ₹1,500 or ₹5,000/month | $99 or $299/month |

Payment 40% / 30% / 30%. Until Nov 2026 the live prices were much lower (Basic $110 / Pro $350 / Advanced $550; ₹8,000 / ₹25,000 / ₹40,000).

- **Where and how:** online only. Leads arrive by email, a quote builder on the site, WhatsApp and the founder's personal network. Planned channels: personalised outreach that includes the prospect's own website speed score, prospecting B2B sellers on IndiaMART, referrals (10% fee), accountant/GST-consultant partners, a LinkedIn company page, Google Business Profile.
- **Constraints:** one founder, part-time (about 15–20 hours a week) alongside a full-time software engineering job; the founder's name may not appear publicly (studio voice only); no stated marketing budget; rule that nothing new is built until a client pays; a personal financial deadline in February 2027. The founder's stated ambition for the project is "1 million in a year" (currency not specified).
- **Assets:** an internal component library used to assemble sites faster; a live studio website with an instant quote builder; a logo and brand system; a LinkedIn company page (9 followers in Sep 2026); 3 vertical demo landing pages (luxury watch, perfume, café).

## Track record

- 3+ client sites shipped: a live B2B chemical-trading company site (Sep 2026, with RFQ/enquiry forms), a photography portfolio, a creator's site, personal portfolio sites. Testimonials from 2 clients. **No published case studies**; Featured Work section on the studio site is switched off.
- Earlier the founder explored and dropped several software products (a webhook reliability tool, an LLM cost gateway, a dental X-ray AI) under the same studio umbrella; one (dental AI) is a dormant outreach-only lead.

## Evidence since the first board

**First board (on the RFQ niche):** 3 FUND IF, average 4.3/10. Shared risks: commodity pricing, no visible proof, focus vs capacity.

**Competitor scan (public sources):**
- Website market: India freelancers about ₹10,000–80,000 (template WordPress ₹5,000–15,000); India agencies ₹50,000–₹2,00,000+; US freelancers about $500–$5,000; US agencies $2,000–$20,000+; marketplaces about $400–$1,200; site builders $10–$39 a month.
- RFQ niche: IndiaMART's paid plan (₹50,000/yr, includes a lead-manager CRM); a specialist selling IndiaMART catalogue care (₹2,500/mo) and catalogue sites (₹39,999); free or cheap tools that already send quotations or rate cards to IndiaMART leads on WhatsApp (a no-code workflow tool, a free CRM app, a ₹7,999/yr WhatsApp tool); billing/quotation apps from free to about ₹3,400/yr. Conclusion of the scan: auto-quote *technology* is not a gap; a done-for-you service on the trader's own channel might be.
- Top public complaint about IndiaMART: fake or shared leads.
- A Google search for "ShapeKraft" returns unrelated "ShapeCraft" results.

**Simulated buyer panel (20 AI-simulated Indian B2B trader owners, shown the RFQ Launch Kit at ₹60,000 + ₹5,000/mo):**
- 2 buy, 18 pass (10%; simulated, so treat as an upper bound).
- Both buyers were established distributors (20–50 staff) with a younger owner modernising, who had lost an order to a faster quote. Micro and small firms: 0 of 16.
- Pass reasons: trust in a small remote studio with one client (7), no need, "repeat buyers just call me" (7), habit (4). Daily/per-customer price changes raised often.
- What would flip a no: a trial on their own price list (12 of 18), proof from the existing client or a similar trader (14 of 18), an ownership/handover clause (4).
- Acceptable setup price across all 20: about ₹15,000–₹50,000; the distributor subgroup called ₹35,000 a bargain and ₹90,000 expensive.

**Unit economics (estimates, not real data):** about $65 / ₹5,500 direct cost per project; fixed costs about $1,875/month (₹1.59 lakh at an assumed ₹85/$) of which $1,500 is a **placeholder** founder draw. Real hours per project, cost to acquire a client, and the real founder draw are unknown.
