# Board brief: ShapeKraft (12 Nov 2026)

## The idea (from founder/idea.md)

- **What it is:** a remote web engineering studio (founded 2025) that builds fast, custom websites in Next.js/React/TypeScript (no templates) and wires AI automation and integrations into them.
- **Who it is for:** small and growing businesses that want a custom, fast site rather than a template, and B2B companies (traders, manufacturers, distributors) that need catalogue/RFQ flows, proposals and ongoing maintenance. Two markets: India (shapekraft.in, prices in INR) and US/global (shapekraft.co, prices in USD).
- **What it sells, at what price (proposed Nov 2026, not yet live):** packages at "founding client" rates for the first 5 clients in each market, in exchange for a case study and testimonial; list prices shown as the anchor.

| Care plan (monthly) | ₹1,500 or ₹5,000 | $99 or $299 |

Payment terms: 40% design / 30% build / 30% launch. The previous live prices were Basic $110 / Pro $350 / Advanced $550 (₹8,000 / ₹25,000 / ₹40,000).

- **Where and how:** online only. Leads today arrive by email, an instant-quote builder on the website, WhatsApp and the founder's personal network. Delivery is remote; the studio assembles sites from an internal component library to deliver faster.
- **Budget and constraints:** solo founder working part-time alongside a full-time software engineering job (so realistic capacity is limited, roughly 15–20 hours a week); the founder's name may not appear on any public surface (studio voice only); no stated marketing budget; a rule that nothing new is built until a client pays; a personal financial deadline in February 2027.

## Other facts a stranger needs

- **Track record:** 3+ sites shipped: a live B2B chemical-trading company site (launched Sep 2026, with lead/RFQ forms), a photography portfolio, a site for a creator, and personal portfolio sites. Testimonials received from 2 clients. **No published case studies yet**; the "Featured Work" section on the studio site is switched off.
- **Proposed core offer:** "Launch + Automate": a fast custom site plus one automation, live in 3 weeks, with a guarantee of 90+ Lighthouse on mobile and enquiries delivered to inbox/WhatsApp, or fixed free. Entry offer: free website speed scan, then a paid audit (₹2,999 / $149) credited to a build.
- **Proposed channels:** personalised outreach that includes the prospect's own website speed score; prospecting B2B sellers on IndiaMART; a 10% referral fee; partnerships with accountants/GST consultants and small marketing agencies; a LinkedIn company page; Google Business Profile and directory listings.
- **Brand facts:** name "ShapeKraft"; a Google search for the name currently returns unrelated "ShapeCraft" results (3D-design research, a 3D-printing shop). Tagline in use: "Engineered, not just designed."
- **Earlier unit-economics estimate (from founder/cfo.md, all inputs assumed):** about $65 of direct cost per project, about $1,875 of fixed monthly cost including an assumed $1,500 founder draw.
- **Public competitor price ranges (from founder/competitors.md, public blogs):** US freelancers about $500–$5,000 for a small business site; US agencies $2,000–$20,000+; India freelancers about ₹10,000–₹80,000 (template WordPress sites ₹5,000–₹15,000); India agencies ₹50,000–₹2,00,000+; Fiverr-style marketplaces about $400–$1,200 for a small business site; builders (Framer, Wix, Webflow) $10–$39 a month.
