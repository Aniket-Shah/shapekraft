## 3. The stack

**Name: ShapeKraft Quote-First Desk** — "get your quote out first, from your own price list, approved by you."

- **Core:** enquiries from IndiaMART, WhatsApp, email and the website become drafted quotes from your own price list, sent to you on WhatsApp in minutes; one tap to approve. Catalogue website included in the full desk.
- **Entry:** 14-day **Quote-First Pilot** on your real price list and real enquiries, ₹5,000, deducted from setup.
- **Bonuses (each kills one objection):**
  1. *Rates by WhatsApp:* update today's rates or a customer's special price by sending a message (kills "prices change daily").
  2. *Done-for-you price list:* we turn your Excel/WhatsApp rates into the desk in one call (kills "no time to teach it").
  3. *Ownership & handover in writing:* domain, code, hosting and data are yours (kills "what if you disappear").
- **Guarantees:** (1) pilot: < 7 in 10 drafts without a price change → ₹5,000 back; (2) after launch: below 8 in 10 → no monthly fee until fixed.
- **Price:** ₹5,000 pilot → ₹60,000 setup (pilot credited) + ₹5,000/mo.
- **Urgency (true):** Founding 5 — the first 5 distributors who continue pay ₹45,000 setup for a published case study; ends **31 Jan 2027**.

## 4. What the guarantees cost (estimates; run `/founder-cfo` with real numbers)

| Item | Assumption | Expected cost per buyer |
|---|---|---:|
| Pilot delivery | ~₹1,500 tooling (one month of a WhatsApp API plan at entry price, per `competitors.md`) + ~8 founder hours | ₹1,500 + time |
| Pilot refund | 30% of pilots claim (assumption) × ₹5,000 | ₹1,500 |
| **Net cash per pilot** | ₹5,000 − ₹1,500 − ₹1,500 | **≈ ₹2,000** (before time) |
| Monthly-fee pause | 30% of clients lose 2 months × ₹5,000 (assumption) | ₹3,000 |
| **Guarantee cost per converted client** | refund share + pause | ≈ ₹4,500 ≈ **8% of the ~₹54,500 a kit leaves after direct costs** |

Affordable on these assumptions. The pilot roughly breaks even in cash; it's a filter and a sales tool, not a profit line. **Risk:** if the desk can't hit 7 in 10 on real changing prices, refunds rise towards 100% — so measure accuracy on the first 3 pilots before promising it widely.

## 6. Re-test (same 20 simulated buyers, same seed 886378)

| | v1 pitch (RFQ Launch Kit) | v2 offer (Quote-First Desk) |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Buy** | **2 of 20 (10%)** | **9 of 20 (45%)** |
| Established distributors (4) | 50% | 75% |
| Small trader-manufacturers (7) | 0% | 57% |
| Micro firms (9) | 0% | 22% |
| Pass for trust | 7 | 3 |
| Pass for need | 7 | 2 |
| Pass for habit | 4 | 5 |
| Acceptable setup range (Van Westendorp) | ₹14,959–₹50,338 | ₹14,988–₹60,038 |

**Read this honestly:**

- **Almost every new "buy" is the ₹5,000 pilot, not the ₹60,000 desk.** Most said they'd decide on the full desk after the pilot; several said they'd still push the setup down to ₹35,000–45,000. So the offer moved people into a cheap trial, which is what it was designed to do — but it hasn't proven the full price.
- **Trust and need objections dropped the most** (7 → 3 and 7 → 2). Habit stayed (5): "phone-and-WhatsApp old school" buyers still bought 0%.
- **The acceptable range's upper end rose** from about ₹50k to about ₹60k, so the list price now sits at the edge, not outside it.
- Simulated buyers lean agreeable; treat 45% as an upper bound for pilot sign-ups, not kit sales.

**New objections the re-test surfaced (fix next):**

1. **"The code handover doesn't help — I'm not a programmer. Who *runs* it if you stop?"** (P005, P007, P020) → add a named backup maintainer or a documented runbook another developer can pick up (solution M).
2. **"My price list is in my head, not in Excel."** (P011, P016, P017) → the pilot must start with a 30-minute price-list call where ShapeKraft captures it; say so explicitly.
3. **"I want quotes, not a website."** (P006, P015) → test an unbundled **Quote Desk only** (lower setup, same monthly), with the website as an optional add-on.
4. **"Will the refund actually be paid?"** (P001, P015) → pay refunds within 48 hours, stated in writing.
