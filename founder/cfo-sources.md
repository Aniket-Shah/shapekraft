# Where the CFO numbers come from

Everything below is an **estimate** unless a source is named. The founder should replace each with a real invoice, quote or bank figure.

| Input | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Average price per project | $740 | Derived from the quote-builder price list in [BUSINESS.md](../BUSINESS.md) with an assumed mix: 25% Basic ($110), 35% Pro ($350), 25% Advanced ($550), 15% B2B at an assumed $1,500; plus 6 pages × $30 on the non-B2B 85% ($153); plus 35% of projects taking an add-on at an average of $217 ($76). Total $512.5 + $153 + $76, rounded to $740. The mix and the B2B price are assumptions. |
| Variable cost per project | $65 | Payment fees at about 3%, hosting/domain pass-through, assets and AI usage. Estimates. |
| Founder draw | $1,500/month | Placeholder. The real figure matters most to the result. |
| Software and other fixed costs | $375/month | Typical subscription and marketing costs for a one-person studio. Estimates. |
| Startup spend | $800 | Registration, brand setup, launch ads and two portfolio builds. Estimate. |
| Volume | 6 projects/month at plan, ramping 1 → 6 over year 1 | Assumed. Not validated by any buyer test. |
| Capacity | 8 projects/month | One builder at about 3–4 days per project plus calls. Assumed. |
