## What would flip a no

- Speaking directly to that chemical trading company owner and seeing it draft a correct quote from my own price list on a real enquiry before I pay anything.
- Seeing it draft correct quotes on my own last month's enquiries for free, plus a setup fee under Rs 25,000, or a call with a trader in my line who says it brought in more orders.
- If a trader like me, in my line, showed me the desk had brought them new paying customers from enquiries they used to miss, and the full price was closer to Rs 25,000 with no monthly fee.
- Setup under Rs 25,000 with a small monthly fee, and proof from a trader like me that faster quotes actually turned into more orders, not just faster replies.
- If someone in my own trade whom I know personally tells me it brought them new IndiaMART buyers that they were losing before, and the monthly fee is cut to around Rs 2,000.
- If a trader in my own line showed me that this got him more IndiaMART orders than he was losing because he replied late, with real numbers, and the monthly fee was much lower.
- Speaking directly to a trader in my own line who has used it for a few months and can tell me it won them orders, with no setup fee up front: just pay monthly once it is working.
- If you show me, for free on my phone, a few real quotes drafted from my own Excel for this week's IndiaMART enquiries with the right prices, and the chemical trader on the reference call tells me it actually won them orders.
- Another distributor in my line that I know personally telling me they use it and it saves them hours every day, plus seeing a correct quote made from my own list right in front of me before I hand anything over.
- Another trader in my line, someone I can actually call, telling me it got him new orders from IndiaMART enquiries he used to miss, and you doing all the price-list setup yourself without taking my time.
- Seeing it in front of me on my own phone: 10 of my real IndiaMART enquiries from this week drafted with the right rate for the right customer, without me first spending days building a price sheet, and a talk with that chemical trader saying it saved him time.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
