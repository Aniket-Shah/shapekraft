## What would flip a no

- Talking directly to that chemical-trading client and seeing the quote desk work on my own price list with my real IndiaMART enquiries before I pay more than a small amount.
- Show me it working on my own Excel price list for two weeks free, with every quote correct after a rate change, and bring the setup down to around Rs 30,000 with the monthly fee only starting once I see more orders.
- Proof from a trader like me that it brought real new orders beyond IndiaMART, and a much lower monthly fee or a free trial on my own enquiries before I pay setup.
- Proof from another trader like me that it brought new orders or got more IndiaMART enquiries converted, with only the quote desk on offer for a low monthly fee and no big setup cost.
- Talking directly to two or three other distributors who have used the quote desk for months, plus a written handover clause that gives me the code, hosting access and price-list data if ShapeKraft shuts down.
- Meeting them face to face or talking to that chemical-trading client directly, plus a written promise that I own the website, domain and price list and can take everything to someone else if they disappear.
- Proof from a trader like me that it brought real new orders, for example a few months of numbers showing more enquiries converted to quotes, and a trial where I pay the monthly only after I see it working.
- Seeing it work live on my own enquiries for two weeks, with per-customer pricing and a way for me to update rates from WhatsApp in seconds, so the drafts are actually right.
- Let me speak to that chemical trading client, and let me try only the quote desk on my real IndiaMART and WhatsApp enquiries for two weeks before I pay anything big for the website.
- If I could try it free for a month on my real IndiaMART enquiries and it actually got more new buyers to reply or saved me real time, with no big setup fee upfront.
- Talking to that chemical-trading client myself and seeing their quote desk actually work, plus a written handover clause giving me the code and the price-list setup if ShapeKraft stops, and setup brought down to around Rs 30,000.
- Someone in my own trade whom I know telling me it got them more orders or saved real time, plus a free trial on my live IndiaMART enquiries for a month before I pay any setup.
