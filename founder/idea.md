# ShapeKraft

- **What it is:** a remote web engineering studio (founded 2025) that builds fast, custom websites in Next.js/React/TypeScript (no templates) and wires AI automation and integrations into them.
- **Who it is for:** small and growing businesses that want a custom, fast site rather than a template, and B2B companies (traders, manufacturers, distributors) that need catalogue/RFQ flows, proposals and ongoing maintenance. India (shapekraft.in, INR) and US/global (shapekraft.co, USD).
- **What it sells, at what price (Nov 2026 proposal, PR #1, not yet live):** website packages at "founding client" rates for the first 5 clients per market, in exchange for a case study, with list prices shown as the anchor:
  - Starter (5 pages): ₹12,000 founding / ₹15,000 list · $450 / $750
  - Growth (10 pages, CMS, lead capture): ₹25,000 / ₹35,000 · $900 / $1,500
  - Studio (15 pages, design system): ₹45,000 / ₹60,000 · $1,800 / $3,000
  - B2B: by scope, from ₹75,000 / $4,000
  - First AI automation workflow: ₹18,000 / ₹30,000 · $700 / $1,500
  - Care plans: ₹1,500 or ₹5,000 a month · $99 or $299 a month
  - Payment 40% design / 30% build / 30% launch.
  - Previous live prices were Basic $110 / Pro $350 / Advanced $550 (₹8,000 / ₹25,000 / ₹40,000).
- **Where and how:** online only. Leads today come from email, the quote builder on the site, WhatsApp and personal network. Delivery is remote, using an internal component library to assemble sites quickly.
- **Budget and constraints:** solo founder working part-time alongside a full-time software engineering job; no founder name on any public surface (studio voice only); no stated marketing budget; "nothing gets built until a client pays"; a personal financial deadline in February 2027. Track record: 3+ sites shipped (a live B2B chemical-trading site, a photography portfolio, a client site for a creator, personal portfolios), testimonials from 2 clients, no published case studies yet.
