## 1. Positioning

Three versions:

1. **For Indian B2B traders and distributors who lose orders because quotes go out slowly**, ShapeKraft is the web studio that turns enquiries into quotes in minutes (a catalogue site plus RFQ → auto-quote), unlike a template site that just collects enquiries in an inbox.
2. For B2B sellers who get enquiries on IndiaMART but run quotes on phone, Excel and WhatsApp, ShapeKraft builds the system that answers every RFQ the same day, unlike hiring another sales executive.
3. For traders who want to look as serious online as they are offline, ShapeKraft builds fast catalogue sites engineered for B2B buyers, unlike local web designers selling WordPress themes.

**Recommended: version 1.** It names the money problem (slow quotes = lost orders), it's the one thing the board says ShapeKraft could own, and it's provable with the live chemical-trading site. Version 3 competes on looks and price, which the board flagged as commodity ground. Evidence is board + one client, not panel data: confirm in calls.

## 3. Ten hooks

Each answers a **panel** objection (simulated buyers; confirm on calls). Format noted. Strongest for the panel: 5 (own price list), 4 + handover clause (trust), 1 (lost-order pain).

1. *("Repeat buyers just call me" — panel #2)* — Outreach opener: "Your repeat buyers call. Your new IndiaMART buyers ask 5 suppliers at once. Who quotes first?"
2. *(Too expensive)* — "One extra order a month pays for the whole system. Here's the maths for your price list." (send the maths)
3. *(WhatsApp is enough)* — LinkedIn post headline: "WhatsApp is where the RFQ arrives. It's also where it gets lost."
4. *(Trust: small studio might vanish — panel #1)* — Outreach line: "You own the domain, code and price list in writing. If we ever stop, you get everything."
5. *(Prices change daily — panel #3)* — "Send your Excel price list. For ₹5,000 we run the quote desk on your real enquiries for 14 days; you update rates from WhatsApp. Credited if you continue."
6. *(Takes too long)* — "Catalogue live in 3 weeks. RFQ automation in week 4." (only once real hours confirm it)
7. *(AI will quote wrong prices)* — "Every auto-quote is drafted for you to approve in one tap. Nothing goes out unchecked."
8. *(Our site is fine)* — Speed-scan opener: "Your site took {x}s to load on a phone. Buyers on mobile leave after 3." (use their real number)
9. *(We're too small for this)* — LinkedIn: "Built for 2-person trading firms, not enterprises. Setup in one call."
10. *(Who are you?)* — "See the chemical-trading site we built: {link}. Ask them how quotes run now." (only with the client's written permission)

## 5. Budget and numbers

- **Budget split:** ₹0 cash. Time: ~60% outreach + follow-ups, 20% partners/warm, 20% LinkedIn/GBP. Only cash cost is the 10% referral fee, paid after a sale.
- **The most you can pay to win one customer:** an RFQ Launch Kit leaves about **₹54,500** after direct costs (setup only; estimate converted at an assumed ₹85/$). Purchases per buyer: **1 setup + the monthly plan** (panel would confirm; assume 1 for safety). Keeping acquisition cost to a third of the setup contribution gives a **ceiling of about ₹18,000 per customer**. The 10% referral fee (₹6,000 at ₹60,000) sits well under that, which is why partners are a good channel.
- **Three numbers to watch weekly:**

| Number | Healthy | Change something if |
|---|---|---|
| Reply rate on outreach | ≥ 15% | < 8% after 20 messages → change hook / list |
| Calls per 10 messages | ≥ 1 | 0 after 20 messages → change list or channel |
| Advance paid per 5 calls | ≥ 1 | 0 after 8 calls → change offer or price (back to `/founder-offer`) |

## Rules followed

No fake reviews, counts or "as seen on". Client names, numbers and screenshots only with written permission. No public founder identity: everything goes out as "ShapeKraft". Speed scores quoted are the prospect's real ones.
