# ShapeKraft: one page

**Verdict: Profitable on estimated numbers. Demand not yet tested.**

**Problem.** Small and growing businesses either build a template site themselves (Framer, Wix, Webflow at about $10 to $39 a month) or pay a freelancer or agency $500 to $20,000+ for a custom one. Few offer a fast custom build with AI workflows and integrations in one place. (Sources in `competitors.md`; the last claim is a hypothesis.)

**Customer.** Founders and small teams who want a site that is custom, fast and connected to their tools, plus B2B companies needing proposals, decks and ongoing maintenance. India and US/global.

**Offer and price.** Packages Basic $110, Pro $350, Advanced $550 (₹8,000, ₹25,000, ₹40,000), $30 (₹2,000) a page, add-ons $150 to $350, B2B by quote, paid 40% design / 30% build / 30% launch. The board recommends testing a higher US ladder ($750 / $1,500 / $3,000).

**Evidence.** Competitor price ranges from public sources; a computed unit-economics model; a board review (2 fund if, 1 pass, average 5.7 out of 10). **No buyer panel and no real customer test yet.**

**What the money is for.** About $800 of startup spend (registration, brand setup, launch ads, two portfolio builds) plus a cash buffer of about $3,725 to carry the first months.

**The ask / next step.** Run the buyer panel (20 buyers for a first look), then test a $1,500 US tier with real prospects. Publish 2 to 3 measured case studies. Narrow the pitch to B2B teams that need a site plus automation.
